SCORING MODEL — FOUR WORK STYLES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Each answer choice is tagged to one of four styles (A / B / C / D).
Every question presents exactly two of the four styles head-to-head.
The user's score for each style accumulates across all 24 choices.
The style(s) with the highest score become the result.
Ties produce a "hybrid" result (two or more styles combined).

PAIRING STRUCTURE (design note)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The 24 questions are structured as 4 rounds of 6 questions each.
Within each round, every possible pair of the four styles
(A/B, A/C, A/D, B/C, B/D, C/D) appears exactly once.
This is a complete balanced paired-comparison design —
every style is matched against every other style equally.

Round   Questions   Theme
─────   ─────────   ──────────────────────────────────────────
1       Q01–Q06     Action vs. collaboration vs. info vs. process
2       Q07–Q12     Outcomes vs. people vs. creativity vs. analysis
3       Q13–Q18     Authority vs. involvement vs. autonomy vs. systems
4       Q19–Q24     Knowing what vs. who vs. why vs. how

RESULT TYPES — DESCRIPTIONS (verbatim from source)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

── A: FOCUSER (Self-Starter) ──────────────────────────────────

As a Focuser, you thrive on understanding the core of tasks and are
naturally inclined to take charge and work independently. You excel
when you have a clear understanding of the objectives and can direct
your efforts towards practical, tangible outcomes. Your leadership
style is authoritative, and you value productivity and efficiency.
Experience is your guide, and you are driven by the influence of
strong leaders. Clear goals provide you with the direction you need
to succeed, and you shine when given the authority to execute your
vision. Hands-on learning is your forte, as you absorb knowledge
best through direct experience. In essence, you are outcome-focused,
value practical solutions, and prefer roles where you can leverage
your independent, self-starting nature to achieve significant results.

── B: RELATOR (Enthusiastic) ──────────────────────────────────

As a Relator, your strength lies in your ability to connect with
people and focus on the whom. You excel in seeing the big picture
and enjoy roles that involve coordinating and facilitating teamwork.
You thrive in environments that emphasize collaboration and democratic
management, where everyone's participation is valued. Flexibility is
important to you, and you adapt well to varying situations by
organizing and fostering group cohesion. You are influenced by the
collective goals of the team and work best with broad objectives
that allow for widespread involvement. Your primary focus is on
fostering participation and creating an inclusive atmosphere where
everyone can contribute. Influence within the group is key to your
approach, and you learn most effectively through observing and
actively participating in group activities. Your enthusiastic nature
and commitment to teamwork make you a valuable asset in any
collaborative setting.

── C: INTEGRATOR (Finisher) ──────────────────────────────────

As an Integrator, your passion lies in understanding the deeper
significance of your work. You excel in roles that involve
problem-solving and diagnosing, constantly seeking innovative
solutions. Your approach to management is self-directed and
methodical, with a strong emphasis on planning. Self-reliance is
crucial for you, and you thrive when given the autonomy to question
and explore. Personal reasoning and thoughtful analysis guide your
decisions, making you adept at working with ideas and input from
various sources. Your focus is on gathering and integrating insights
to create meaningful outcomes. You prefer having ample time to
assess situations and learn best through listening and absorbing
information. Your analytical mindset and dedication to understanding
the why behind tasks make you a valuable asset in any environment
that values innovation and thoroughness.

── D: OPERATOR (Detailer) ─────────────────────────────────────

As an Operator, your expertise lies in understanding the intricacies
of your work. You excel in roles that involve monitoring and
analyzing, with a keen eye for details. Your meticulous approach
ensures that every aspect is documented accurately, and you manage
with a systematic and controlled style. Accountability is paramount
for you, and you place high value on compliance and adhering to
established policies. You thrive in structured environments where
systems and procedures are well-defined. Your focus is on
maintaining order and ensuring that boundaries are respected. You
learn best through repetition, which helps you master processes and
achieve consistency. Your methodical nature and dedication to
precision make you indispensable in roles that require thorough
analysis and detailed execution.

IMPLEMENTATION NOTES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

- Single-select forced choice per question (no skipping, no neutral).
- Auto-advances after a short delay (~480 ms) once an option is picked.
- Progress shown as "Question N of 24" + percentage bar.
- Score state: { A: 0, B: 0, C: 0, D: 0 } — increment on each pick.
- Result = style(s) where score === Math.max(...all scores).
- "Take it again" resets all scores and returns to intro screen.
