RESULT TYPE DEFINITIONS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

R — REALISTIC / "Doers"
  Likes:   Work that involves designing, building, or repairing of
           equipment, materials, or structures, engaging in physical
           activity, or working outdoors.
  Thrives: In environments where hands-on problem solving is valued,
           where the results of your work are tangible and visible,
           and where you can engage directly with tools, materials,
           machines, or the natural world.

I — INVESTIGATIVE / "Thinkers"
  Likes:   Work that involves studying and researching non-living
           objects, living organisms, disease or other forms of
           impairment, or human behavior.
  Thrives: In environments where curiosity is rewarded, where you
           are given the space to ask questions, analyse data, and
           develop evidence-based conclusions, and where intellectual
           rigour is the standard.

A — ARTISTIC / "Creators"
  Likes:   Work that involves creating original visual artwork,
           performances, written works, food, or music for a variety
           of media, or applying artistic principles to the design
           of various objects and materials.
  Thrives: In environments where creative freedom is respected,
           where original thinking is celebrated over conformity,
           and where your work has the opportunity to move, inspire,
           or provoke a response in others.

S — SOCIAL / "Helpers"
  Likes:   Work that involves helping, teaching, advising, assisting,
           or providing service to others.
  Thrives: In environments where human connection is at the centre
           of the work, where your ability to listen, empathise, and
           support others is genuinely valued, and where you can see
           the direct impact of your efforts on the people around you.

E — ENTERPRISING / "Persuaders"
  Likes:   Work that involves managing, negotiating, marketing, or
           selling, typically in a business setting, or leading or
           advising people in political and legal situations.
  Thrives: In environments where ambition is rewarded, where you
           have the opportunity to lead, influence, and drive
           outcomes, and where the stakes are high enough to keep
           you genuinely engaged.

C — CONVENTIONAL / "Organizers"
  Likes:   Work that involves following procedures and regulations
           to organize information or data, typically in a business
           setting.
  Thrives: In environments where structure and precision are valued,
           where clear processes and systems exist to guide your
           work, and where your ability to maintain order, accuracy,
           and consistency makes a measurable difference.

SCORING LOGIC (from source)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

- Sum all Likert values (1–4) for each Holland type.
- Rank all 6 types by total score, highest first.
- The top 3 types form the user's Holland Code (3-letter code).
- Holland Code is conventionally written in uppercase (e.g. SAE, RIC).
- Ties: the app presents both type names with equal weight.

Progress bar: 42 steps mapped non-linearly to 0–100% — the bar
accelerates at first to feel responsive, then slows near the end
(PROGRESS_MAP array in source).
