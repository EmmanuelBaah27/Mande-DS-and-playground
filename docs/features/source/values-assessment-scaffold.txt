CAT 0 — How You Like to Work (10 questions)
──────────────────────────────

1. Fast Pace
   "Think about a time you were racing against a deadline — heart pumping,
   moving fast, getting things done at full speed. How essential is that kind
   of intensity to you feeling alive at work?"

2. Tranquility
   "Think about a time you worked without pressure — your own pace, no one
   rushing you, calm all around. How essential is that kind of ease to you
   doing your best work?"

3. Pressure
   "Think about a time the stakes were high — no room for mistakes, real
   consequences, people depending on you. How essential is that level of
   pressure to keeping you sharp and motivated?"

4. Autonomy
   "Think about a time you got to decide what to work on and when — no one
   dictating your schedule or your to-do list. How essential is that kind of
   full freedom to you thriving at work?"

5. Structured Tasks
   "Think about a time everything was clearly laid out — your tasks defined,
   your priorities set, no guesswork. How essential is that kind of clarity
   to you performing at your best?"

6. Variety & Change
   "Think about a time your work kept switching things up — new tasks, new
   settings, new challenges every few days. How essential is that kind of
   constant change to you staying engaged?"

7. Predictability
   "Think about a time you always knew what was coming — same kind of tasks,
   reliable outcomes, no surprises. How essential is that kind of consistency
   to you feeling settled and productive?"

8. Precision
   "Think about a time the details really mattered — exact standards, careful
   work, no cutting corners. How essential is that level of precision to you
   feeling proud of what you produce?"

9. Adventure
   "Think about a time your work involved real risk — bold decisions, uncertain
   outcomes, going where others wouldn't. How essential is that kind of boldness
   to you feeling fulfilled?"

10. Safety
    "Think about a time you worked in an environment where you knew you were
    physically safe — no danger, no risk of harm. How essential is that kind
    of security to you showing up fully?"

SCORING SUMMARY
────────────────────────────────────────

Each question is scored 1–4:
  4 = Can't work without it
  3 = Really matters to me
  2 = Would be nice
  1 = Doesn't do it for me

Skipped questions are excluded from scoring.

Category score = sum of scores for all answered questions in that category.
Higher score = stronger priority for that value domain.
