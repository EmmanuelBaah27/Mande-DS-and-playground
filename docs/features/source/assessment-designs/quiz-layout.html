B — Side by side

Two cards next to each other with OR in the middle. Works nicely for short labels but struggles with longer ones like "Know what needs to be done; then cut loose and do it." Text wrapping gets awkward.

Note on longer answers

Some options are quite long — e.g. "Know what needs to be done; then cut loose and do it" vs "Know who else will be included or affected." The layout needs to handle both short and long text gracefully.
